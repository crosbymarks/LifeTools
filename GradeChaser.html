ℹ️ About Grade Chaser

Grade Chaser is an interactive educational RPG simulation designed to teach students the mathematical consequences of missed assignments, grade weights, and academic standing.

  • The Zero Trap: Mathematically demonstrates how taking a single zero causes immense cumulative grade drag compared to submitting partial work.
  • Custom Rules Engine: Customize category weights (e.g. 55/45), formative/summative grade brackets, and make-up caps via the Settings menu.
  • Pace Modes: Choose Standard Mode (2 formatives/week) or Quick Mode (1 grade/week).
  • No-Pass, No-Play: Simulates varsity athletic eligibility rules with realistic probation periods and benched game days.
  • Sandbox Simulator: Interactive post-game sandbox lets you directly click graph bars to test "What If" scenarios and see grade changes instantly.
