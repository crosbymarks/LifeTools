Sponsor Info & Game Goals

Why we play and how the incentives work

🎯 The Core Goal

Learn the timeless power of compound interest, how to build a cash-flow defense against unexpected life storms, and the foundational habit of paying yourself first.

📜 Rules of the Journey
  • Discretionary Pay: You receive $500/mo ($6,000/yr) after living bills and a modest 10% for entertainment.
  • Life Shocks: You face a 60% chance each year of a major recurring expense that will reduce your monthly paycheck for that entire year.
  • Debt Consequence: If you allocate more than you earn, Checking goes negative and bleeds $10/mo in overdraft fees until cleared.
💰 Sponsor Incentive Structure
  • Real Cash Payout: Final net savings will be converted and paid out in real life at a ratio of 2000 game dollars = $1 real dollar.
  • Charitable Giving: Any game money donated to Charity is also converted at the same ratio, and then multiplied by 5x as a real-world donation by your Sponsor!
